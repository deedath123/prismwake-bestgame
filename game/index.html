PRISMWAKE

Steer the beacon's beam through turning mirrors and burn the Murk back.
Seven nights. Three tower kinds. Nine ember upgrades. The beam moves while the night does.
Goal — hold the beacon for 7 nights. If the Murk reaches it, its light dies a little.
Build — pick a tower (keys 1–3), click a stone plot. Mirrors bend the beam; prisms split it; lanterns shine alone.
Steer live — during the assault, click any mirror and turn it with Q / E or the mouse wheel. No pausing the night to think.
Burn — Murk caught in the light burns and pays embers. Between nights, spend them in the ember shop.
Keys — Space begins the night, P pauses, R restarts, right-click sells a tower for 60% back.
